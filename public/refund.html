Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Scholarship coupons

Coupons are issued after the test, are valid for 24 hours, and have no cash value.

Program fee

Once a payment is successful and the seat is confirmed, the fee is non-refundable, except in the cases below.

When we refund

  • The Company cancels or reschedules the Program and you cannot attend: full refund.
  • You are charged twice or the payment succeeds but no enrollment is created: full refund of the extra or unmatched amount.

Cancellation by the student

Requests made at least 1 day before 8 November 2026 may be considered at our discretion.

Failed payments

If money is debited but the payment fails, the amount is returned automatically by the bank or Razorpay, usually within 5-7 business days.

How to request

Email plantgreeninertia@gmail.com with your registered email and payment reference. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
