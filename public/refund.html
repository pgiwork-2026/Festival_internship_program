Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: Winter 2026

Scholarship Coupons

Scholarship coupons generated upon completion of the scholarship test are non-transferable, carry no cash value, and are valid for redemption within 24 hours of issue.

Student Contribution & Program Fees

Student contributions (e.g. ₹999 or ₹1,199 with an assessment scholarship coupon, or ₹9,999 standard fee) secure your seat, batch mentor allotment, and curriculum materials. Once paid and confirmed, program fees are generally non-refundable except under the specific circumstances listed below.

Eligible Refund Conditions

  • Program Cancellation or Major Schedule Shift: In the unlikely event that Plant Green Inertia cancels or substantially postpones an assigned cohort and you cannot attend the rescheduled dates, you will receive a 100% full refund.
  • Duplicate Deductions: If technical errors result in your bank account being debited more than once for the same enrollment, the duplicate payment will be refunded in full.
  • Payment Captured Without Seat Allotment: If your payment is captured by Razorpay but fails to generate an active enrollment due to server-side synchronization errors, the unmatched payment will be refunded.

Cancellation Requests Prior to Cohort Start

Requests for cancellation submitted in writing at least 48 hours prior to the batch commencement date may be reviewed and considered on a case-by-case basis at the Company’s sole discretion.

Automatic Bank Reversals for Failed Transactions

If money is debited from your bank account during a failed checkout attempt, the funds are reversed automatically by your banking institution or Razorpay within 5–7 business days.

How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, send an email to plantgreeninertia@gmail.com from your registered email address with your full name, mobile number, and Razorpay payment reference ID. Approved refunds are credited directly to the original payment source within 5–7 business days.
