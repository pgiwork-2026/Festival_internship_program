Frequently Asked Questions

3 common topics
01 When are attendance hours & assignment reviews updated?

Cohort attendance hours and milestone evaluations are updated directly in your Dashboard and Submissions tabs once your mentor or coordinator logs session records.

02 How do I download my Completion Certificate?

Open the Certificates tab once you log 30 verified training hours, maintain at least 75% attendance, and Admin issues your credential ID and PDF.

03 Can I update my temporary Date-of-Birth login password?

Yes. Use the Change Login Password card above with your current DOB password (DDMMYYYY) to set a new custom password of at least 8 characters.
