Assessment rules

  • One scholarship attempt per student. The test lasts 20 minutes. The timer runs on our server and cannot be paused; refreshing the page does not reset it.
  • Answers are saved automatically as you select them. The test submits automatically when time ends.
  • Your camera must stay on and you must stay alone in front of it. Before the test your face is verified; during the test we check for no face, more than one person, a different person, head turned away, a mobile phone in view, leaving the tab or full screen, and copy/paste. Looking down for a long time and sustained voices or loud noise are also logged for review; they do not count as warnings.
  • Violations: you get 3 warnings. The 4th violation submits your test automatically with the answers saved so far. A report is generated after the test.
  • Privacy: video is analysed inside your browser and is not recorded. When an event is flagged, one small still photo is saved with it for review by the program team only (at most 8 per attempt). Your microphone is used only to measure loudness; no audio is recorded or uploaded.
  • Your score decides your scholarship: 0–49% = 85% off (₹1,499.85), 50–99% = 90% off (₹999.90), 100% = free (₹0), on the ₹9,999 internship fee. Your coupon is valid for 24 hours.
