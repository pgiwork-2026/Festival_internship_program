Last updated: Winter 2026

These terms govern participation in the Winter Internship and Training Program (“Program”) organized by Plant Green Inertia Pvt. Ltd. (“Company”, “we”, “our”). By registering, taking the assessment, or paying the student contribution fee, you agree to comply with these terms.

1. The Program Overview

The Program is an intensive 30-hour professional curriculum combining interactive training, practical development exercises, and career mentorship. The program is offered across two operational modes: Online (interactive live cohort) and Offline (in-person at our corporate development centre in Chennai).

2. Eligibility & Selection Assessment

  • Admission into the Program is selection-based to maintain quality and cohort engagement.
  • Applicants must complete the 100-mark proctored online scholarship assessment.
  • A computer (laptop/desktop) with a functional webcam and modern browser is required to ensure proctoring integrity.
  • Candidates who do not qualify on their first attempt may re-apply after the scheduled cooling-off period.

3. Scholarship Fees & Contributions

The standard corporate program valuation is ₹9,999. Based on your scholarship assessment performance (25 questions, 100 marks), tuition funding is awarded as follows:

  • 4 to 25 Correct Answers (16% – 100% Score): Merit Scholarship (90% OFF) – ₹999 Student Contribution.
  • 0 to 3 Correct Answers (0% – 12% Score): Foundation Scholarship (88% OFF) – ₹1,199 Student Contribution.

Assigned scholarship coupons are unique to each student, valid for 24 hours from the time of issue, and strictly non-transferable.

4. Secure Payments

Student contributions are securely processed via Razorpay. The Company does not store, transmit, or retain complete credit card numbers, debit card details, or UPI credentials on its servers.

5. Batch Schedules & Timings

Standard session timings are scheduled as:

  • Online Cohort: 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM IST
  • Offline Cohort: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST

Cohort start and end dates are determined by the active batch schedule configured in our administrative portal. Final batch allotment rests with the Company.

6. Academic Integrity & Proctoring

Candidates must take the assessment honestly without unauthorized assistance, tab-switching, or impersonation. Violations of test rules flagged during proctoring may result in attempt cancellation or disqualification without refund.

7. Certification & Career Opportunities

An official, verified Certificate of Internship is awarded upon successful completion of the 30-hour curriculum and practical assignments. Offline participants are eligible for Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) evaluation based on active performance and current vacancies.

The Company reserves the right to modify schedules or syllabus modules to improve curriculum delivery. Updates will be reflected on this portal.
