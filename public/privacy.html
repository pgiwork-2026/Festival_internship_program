Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Plant Green Inertia Pvt. Ltd. respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address and phone number.
  • Test data: answers, scores and attempt details.
  • Proctoring data: webcam images or snapshots and related events captured during the test.
  • Payment status and order references (card, UPI or bank details are handled by Razorpay, not stored by us).

How we use it

  • To run and score the scholarship test and keep it fair.
  • To issue coupons, process enrollment and send program updates by email.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers needed to run the Program (such as hosting, database and Razorpay payments) or when required by law.

Retention and security

Data is stored with reasonable security safeguards and kept only as long as needed for the Program, records and legal purposes. Proctoring images are kept only for review and are automatically deleted once your enrollment/payment is completed.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to plantgreeninertia@gmail.com.
