Privacy Policy

Last updated: Winter 2026

Plant Green Inertia Pvt. Ltd. (“Company”, “we”, “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of students and applicants participating in the Winter Internship and Training Program. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect and how we safeguard it.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Full name, verified email address, and mobile number provided during registration.
  • Assessment Records: Submitted answers, scoring percentages, completion timestamps, and integrity logs.
  • Proctoring Telemetry: Webcam telemetry data, browser focus events, and temporary verification snapshots captured strictly during active test sessions.
  • Payment Identifiers: Razorpay transaction identifiers, order IDs, and payment verification timestamps (financial payment details like card numbers and UPI pins are handled directly by Razorpay).

How We Use Collected Data

  • To administer, evaluate, and score the scholarship assessment fairly and transparently.
  • To verify student identity and calculate applicable merit scholarship discounts.
  • To issue verifiable Certificates of Internship and generate project reports.
  • To communicate cohort schedules, mentor session links, and onboarding instructions.

Data Protection & Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared exclusively with necessary operational infrastructure providers (e.g., Supabase authentication/database hosting, Razorpay payment processing) strictly for program execution, or when required by statutory authorities.

Data Retention & Auto-Purging

Student academic and certificate records are retained for credential validation. In accordance with our privacy commitment, proctoring evidence snapshots captured during tests are automatically purged from our servers within 7 days of assessment completion.

Student Rights & Support

You have the right to request access to or correction of your profile records. For any privacy queries or data deletion requests, contact our privacy officer at plantgreeninertia@gmail.com.
